# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Developers who already work with a coding agent every day (Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode or Pi), on a Mac, in a
terminal. They arrive from a post, a repository or a search, skeptical of yet another agent tool, and decide in
seconds whether this is worth installing. Their job on the site: understand what Aqorin is (one workflow, any of
those runtimes), see that it does not promise more than it does, and install it with npm, then use the
documentation as a reference while they work.

## Product Purpose

The landing explains Aqorin and gets a developer from curiosity to `npm i -g @aqorin/cli`; the documentation at
`/docs` is where they come back to while using it. Success is a visitor who installs it knowing its limits (macOS on
Apple silicon, what the sandbox does not cover, what is advisory), and who finds the answer in the docs without
leaving them. The site follows the product's own rule: never claim a capability or a platform that has not been
demonstrated.

## Brand Personality

Precise, honest, calm. A serious tool that says what it does and what it does not, with confidence and no hype. The
voice is plain and exact; the limits are said in the same tone as the features, not hidden in small print and not
dramatized. The emotional goal is trust: "these people tell me the truth about their tool."

Reference lane: Ghostty and Zed, terminal tools made with care, where the product's own interface (here, the TUI)
is the main image and the page carries little ornament.

## Anti-references

- The current site's "vibecoded technical editorial" look: cream paper with a dot screen and an orange accent;
  small monospace uppercase labels everywhere (`§01`, `ILLUSTRATIVE`, a spec strip, an event-log ticker); huge
  condensed all-caps headlines with a colored period; decorative diagrams (a patch bay of cables, a step line with
  dots) that look like a template.
- Generic AI-tool landing pages: gradient heroes, glowing orbs, hero metrics, identical icon-card grids,
  glassmorphism.
- Hype copy: "supercharge", "10x", claims the records do not back.
- Stock Starlight with only the accent changed: docs that feel unrelated to the landing.

## Design Principles

1. **Show the real thing.** The TUI and real CLI output are the imagery; nothing illustrative that could be mistaken
   for the product.
2. **Say the limits plainly.** What is not demonstrated or not enforced is part of the design, given the same care
   as the features, never a footnote or a warning badge.
3. **Quiet confidence.** One clear idea per section, generous space, no ornament that does not explain something.
4. **One family.** The landing and the docs read as one product: same type, same colors, same brand mark.
5. **Respect the reader's time.** A developer should understand what Aqorin is and how to install it in the first
   screen.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA throughout (text and UI contrast, focus visible, keyboard navigation); `prefers-reduced-motion`
respected (no motion that carries meaning without a static equivalent); light and dark themes, both AA; English
and Spanish at parity.
